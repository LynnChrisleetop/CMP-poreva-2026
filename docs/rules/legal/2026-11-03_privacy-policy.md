1. Introduction

This Policy explains, throughout your registration, participation, use of platform functions, award claims, and communications with us on the Poreva Open platform (the “Platform”), what personal information we collect, for what purposes, with whom it is shared, how it is stored and protected, what rights you have, and how to exercise them. We strive to present this in a clear and readable manner; you may also contact us through the channels listed in Section 15 of this Policy for assistance.

2. Definitions

Personal information: all kinds of information recorded by electronic or other means relating to identified or identifiable natural persons, excluding anonymized information.

Sensitive personal information: personal information that, once leaked or illegally used, may easily lead to infringement of a natural person's dignity or harm to personal or property safety, including biometric data, religious beliefs, specific identity, medical and health information, financial accounts, and location tracks, as well as the personal information of minors under the age of 14.

Processing: includes the collection, storage, use, processing, transmission, provision, disclosure, and deletion of personal information.

Platform: the Poreva Open platform, including its official website, API interfaces, mobile applications (if any), and officially operated user service channels such as WeChat official accounts and WeCom groups.

Participant: an individual or team representative who completes registration under the Official Competition Terms, submits a protein design proposal, and enters into a participation relationship with the Platform.

8. Cross-Border Transfer

Core data of the Poreva Open platform (accounts, submissions, activity, awards, tax) is stored within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

Personal information leaves mainland China only in the following limited scenarios, strictly in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China:

1. CDN and DNS services: a CDN using nodes outside mainland China (candidate: Cloudflare) will inevitably receive your IP address and User-Agent. This is necessary to provide the service. If such CDN does not offer equivalent functionality within mainland China, we will display a prominent notice on the registration page and provide a domestic alternative.

2. Code hosting: if the platform code is open-sourced to GitHub in the future, the code itself contains no personal information; if a contributor writes personal information into a git commit, the contributor is responsible for redacting it.

3. Email delivery outside mainland China: only when the email address you provide points to an overseas service (such as Gmail) does email delivery constitute a cross-border transfer. Such cross-border transfer is not automatically presumed to be consented to by your providing an overseas email address; it will be carried out only after your separate, explicit confirmation. If you do not agree to such cross-border transfer, please provide an email address within mainland China to receive all notifications. If you choose to confirm the use of an overseas email address, you are deemed to be fully aware of and to consent to the foregoing cross-border transfer of personal information.

For the above outbound transfers, we will conduct a personal information protection impact assessment (PIPIA) in accordance with the law and, as required, complete the filing of the standard contract for cross-border transfer with, or the security assessment organized by, the national cyberspace authority.

12. Security Measures

We establish technical and organizational measures in accordance with GB/T 35273 and ISO/IEC 27001, mainly including:

Technical: TLS 1.2+ in transit; AES-256 at rest; Argon2id password hashing; key management provided by a licensed cloud KMS; access granted on a least-privilege basis with audit logs

Organizational: tiered approval for internal access; employee confidentiality agreements and background checks; quarterly security training; annual penetration testing and vulnerability remediation

Incident response: report personal information breach incidents to the cyberspace administration authority within 72 hours (Article 42(2) of the Cybersecurity Law), and notify affected users in parallel

13. Minors
