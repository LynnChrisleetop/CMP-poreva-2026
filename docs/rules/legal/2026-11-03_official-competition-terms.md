Introduction

This “Poreva Open — Global AI Protein Design Challenge” (the “Competition”) is initiated and hosted by MegaRobo Technologies (“MegaRobo” or the “Organizer”); Tencent Cloud, Twist, Galateck, Vazyme, Guangyao Digital Science & Technology, Silang, Future Capital, Inno Angel Fund (collectively, the “Partners”) provide collaborative support to the Competition in their respective fields of expertise. The Organizer and the Partners are hereinafter referred to individually as the “Organizer” or a “Partner” and collectively as the “Parties.”

This Competition and all competition submissions are governed by, and construed in accordance with, the following documents:

(a) these Official Competition Terms (the “Terms”);

(b) the “Poreva Open — Global AI Protein Design Challenge: Challenge Brief and Rules” published together with each challenge (the “Challenge Documents”), which are incorporated into these Terms by reference and apply only within the scope of the corresponding challenge;

(c) the Poreva Open Platform User Guide and the “Poreva Open Privacy Policy” ([link to be added before release]), which are incorporated into these Terms by reference.

In case of any inconsistency among the above documents on a matter specific to a challenge, the Challenge Documents prevail; for all other matters, these Terms prevail.

“Participant” or “you” means a participating team that submits design sequences to the Competition in the name of a team in accordance with the conditions set out in these Terms and the Challenge Documents; where a participating team competes under the auspices of an institution, company, or other entity, “Participant” in these Terms also includes that entity. This edition of the Competition does not accept individual participation, nor does it accept anonymous participation. Each participating team must designate a captain (team representative); the captain's acts of registering the account, accepting these Terms, and submitting competition designs are binding on the team and all of its members.

Clause 1 — Relationships Among the Organizers

1.1 Identity of the Organizer. MegaRobo is the organizer of this Competition. Unless otherwise agreed in these Terms, the Organizer assumes full responsibility for these Terms and this Competition and has the right to decide independently all matters relating to this Competition.

1.2 Responsibilities of the Organizer. MegaRobo is responsible for all organization and implementation of this Competition, including but not limited to: operation of the Poreva Open platform, management of participant registration, validation of targets and assay methods, formulation and execution of design selection criteria and review rules, wet-lab expression and experimental characterization carried out through its Megalaxy Lab, and publication of competition results. The Organizer bears financial and organizational responsibility for all awards, prizes, and supporting services of this Competition. To support open collaboration and public validation in the field of protein design, the Organizer will provide participants with free wet-lab validation services for the designs selected in each round; the specific selection scale, experimental items, and coverage are subject to the relevant Challenge Documents and platform announcements.

1.3 Partners. (a) The Partners provide collaborative support for this Competition in their respective fields of expertise; the specific content, scope, and manner of cooperation between the Partners and the Organizer shall be separately agreed by the parties concerned, and these Terms do not govern such specific arrangements. (b) The Partners are not parties to these Terms; these Terms do not constitute any warranty by the Organizer to Participants in respect of any act, product, or service of a Partner, nor do they impose joint and several liability on any Partner toward Participants. (c) Any matter arising between a Partner and a Participant in connection with this Competition shall be handled by the relevant parties in accordance with the law and their respective agreements.

1.4 Third-Party Service Providers. The Organizer may engage third parties to provide services such as DNA synthesis, gene sequencing, cloud computing, or logistics for the Competition. Such third parties are not the Organizer and owe no obligations to Participants under these Terms; the services they provide to the Competition are subject to their own terms, and actual provision depends on their availability and capacity.

Clause 2 — Binding Decisions

By completing registration and/or participating in this Competition, you agree to be bound by the Organizer's decisions. The Organizer has sole discretion to make all decisions relating to: design selection, review and award determination, wet-lab validation arrangements, results publication, eligibility determination, and handling of violations. All such decisions are internally final and binding on Participants.

Clause 3 — Eligibility

3.1 General Eligibility. This Competition is open to teams and organizations worldwide, regardless of academic or commercial affiliation; participation must be in the name of a team. This edition of the Competition does not accept individual participation, nor does it accept anonymous participation. Requirements on the number, identity, and composition of team members are subject to the relevant Challenge Documents and platform announcements. Each participating team (and the entity under whose auspices it competes) must satisfy all of the following:

(a) all team members must be at least 18 years of age, or have reached the higher legal age of majority in their place of residence (whichever is higher); and the institution, company, or other entity under whose auspices the team competes must be a duly established and validly existing entity;

(b) neither the participating team nor its members or auspicing entity is an individual, team, or entity prohibited from participating in this Competition, receiving awards, or receiving related services under United Nations Security Council resolutions, the laws of the People's Republic of China, the laws of their place of residence, or other applicable laws; where the laws of the place of residence of any team member or of the auspicing entity restrict or prohibit such competition activities, the team may not participate;

(c) the team's participation, and each member's participation in the team, does not violate any law, regulation, employment contract, institutional policy, or third-party right binding on the team or that member.

Employees of the Organizer and its affiliates who are directly involved in the review, selection, or validation work of this Competition, as well as the teams to which they belong, may not participate; other staff members who participate shall proactively disclose their identity at registration and recuse themselves from review-related processes. If any of the above persons is among a team's members, the team shall proactively disclose this at registration and apply for recusal arrangements; the Organizer has the right, as circumstances warrant, to require the person to withdraw from the team or to disqualify the team.

3.2 Representations and Warranties. Each participating team (acting through its team representative) represents and warrants to the Organizer that:

(a) the participating team has obtained all consents and approvals necessary for any employer, institution, or team member to participate in this Competition;

(b) the participating team's submission is its original work, or the team otherwise holds all rights necessary to submit and publish it;

(c) the participating team's participation and its compliance with these Terms do not violate any law, regulation, or third-party right;

(d) where participation will bind an institution or company, the team representative is authorized to accept these Terms on behalf of that institution or company and to make the representations and warranties in this Clause on behalf of all team members;

(e) the registration and participation information provided by the participating team is true, accurate, and complete;

(f) the team representative has obtained the acknowledgment and consent of all team members to these Terms, the Challenge Documents, and the platform rules; all team members meet the eligibility requirements of Clause 3.1, and the team's composition and size comply with the relevant Challenge Documents and platform announcements.

Clause 4 — Registration and Account

4.1 Registration. Participation in this Competition requires completing registration in the name of a team on the Poreva Open platform; registration constitutes acceptance of these Terms. A team must designate a captain (team representative) as its contact and responsible representative, and the captain must be a member of the team; the captain is accountable to the Organizer for the participation of the team and all its members, and shall ensure that all members' acknowledgment of and consent to these Terms, the Challenge Documents, and the platform rules have been obtained. The team shall register with an email address that can prove the identity of the team or its affiliated institution; anonymous participation is not accepted. Requirements on team size and composition are subject to the relevant Challenge Documents and platform announcements.

4.2 Account. Each participating team may register and use only one account, and may not register, borrow, or use multiple accounts in any manner, nor obtain duplicate submission quotas through multiple accounts; team members may not separately register accounts in their personal names to submit designs for this Competition—violations will not be deemed submissions of the team, and the Organizer has the right to handle them under Clause 5.7. The account is held by the team's representative (captain), who is responsible for safeguarding account security; losses caused by the account holder's failure to safeguard the account are borne by the team, except where caused by the Organizer's fault.

Clause 5 — Competition Format

5.1 Track. This Competition has a single unified track and is not divided by participant background. Participants may use any lawful and compliant methods and tools to complete their designs, including but not limited to self-developed models, open-source methods, or commercial AI tools; the Competition neither requires, recommends, nor prohibits any specific tool, and the choice of tool does not affect eligibility or review outcomes. Participants shall truthfully disclose their design methods as required by the Challenge Documents.

5.2 Challenges and Targets. This Competition comprises five challenges. The target, submission specifications, and review and selection criteria of each challenge are subject to the relevant Challenge Document, which is incorporated into these Terms by reference. The Organizer reserves the right to update the Challenge Documents; updates take effect upon publication on the platform.

5.3 Schedule. The Competition proceeds in two rounds, with five challenges released in total, and a submission window of one month per round. All times are Beijing time:

| Round | Challenges | Challenge Release | Submission Deadline for the Round |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Round 1 | Challenge 1, Challenge 2, Challenge 3 | October 18, 2026 | November 18, 2026 |
| Round 2 | Challenge 4, Challenge 5 | November 8, 2026 | December 8, 2026 |

Notes:

(a) the submission format, quantity cap, selection process, and award arrangements for each challenge are subject to the relevant Challenge Document;

(b) arrangements for wet-lab validation and for the publication of results and data are subject to platform announcements;

(c) the above schedule is the Organizer's planned arrangement, and the Organizer reserves the right to adjust it; adjustments take effect upon notification by platform announcement, and submissions completed before an adjustment are handled in accordance with the announcement.

5.4 Submission and Review. Participants submit designs through the Poreva Open platform in accordance with the requirements published in each Challenge Document. The cap on submissions per team for each challenge is subject to the Challenge Document. The Organizer will determine, from compliant submissions and in accordance with the selection process published on the platform, the designs that enter wet-lab validation.

5.5 Biosafety and Sequence Compliance Screening. Every design proposed for wet-lab synthesis and validation will undergo biosafety and sequence compliance screening before synthesis, under criteria consistent with the protocols adopted by the Organizer and its DNA synthesis service providers. Submitted sequences must not encode functional sequences of known toxins or pathogenic factors, or sequences whose synthesis, storage, or handling is prohibited by applicable law. The Organizer has the right to reject or cancel any submission that fails screening or raises biosafety concerns, and is under no obligation to explain the basis for rejection.

5.6 No Guarantee of Testing or Results. Submitting a design does not guarantee that the design will be selected for wet-lab validation, and validation does not guarantee any particular result. The Organizer does not guarantee that any Participant will receive an award or a favorable outcome.

5.7 Fair Play. Participants must not: (a) use multiple accounts to obtain extra submission quotas or manipulate review statistics; (b) interfere with or attack the platform, or probe or scrape other participants' data; (c) submit content that infringes third-party rights; (d) embed in submissions instructional content intended to manipulate the Organizer's review tools (including AI-assisted tools); (e) engage in any other conduct that undermines the fairness of the Competition; (f) where any member of a participating team commits any of the foregoing violations or dishonest acts, such act is deemed a violation by the team. The Organizer has the right to take measures against violations, including disqualification, revocation of awards, account suspension, and public disclosure of the violation, and to retain the relevant submissions as the basis for handling the violation.

Depending on the severity of the violation, the Organizer has the right to take measures against the Participant including but not limited to disqualifying submissions, voiding competition results, revoking awards, suspending accounts, and publicly disclosing the violation, and to retain the relevant submissions as the basis for handling the violation. In the case of team participation, if any team member is found to have committed any dishonest or violating act listed in this Clause, all competition results of the team shall be voided and any awards obtained shall be revoked. The team representative confirms that, at the time of registration and submission of entries, the acknowledgment and consent of all team members to this joint-liability rule have been obtained.

Clause 6 — Results, Publication, and Ownership

6.1 Publication of Results. The Organizer will publish experimental results on the Poreva Open platform.

6.2 Open License. The organizers will screen the submitted entries and experimental verification data, selecting certain designs and their corresponding experimental results (hereinafter referred to as 'released materials') to be made publicly available under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC‑BY 1.0). The specific scope of the data to be publicly disclosed and the selection criteria will be determined by the organizers in due course and announced on the platform. By submitting a design, participants agree that the organizers may release the selected materials as part of the competition results and make them publicly available under ODC‑BY 1.0. Participants acknowledge that not all submitted sequences or experimental results will be publicly disclosed.

Clause 7 — Awards, Prizes, and Taxation

7.1 Awards. The award structure, evaluation criteria, prize content, and distribution arrangements for each challenge are subject to the relevant Challenge Documents and platform announcements. The granting of an award does not constitute any warranty by the Organizer as to the scientific validity, safety, or commercial value of a design.

7.2 Taxation. All tax obligations arising from competition prizes are borne by the winning team and its members. The Organizer handles payment separately according to the recipient of the prize money:

(1) Where the winning team competes under the auspices of an institution, company, or other entity and claims the prize in that entity's name: the Organizer will not withhold individual income tax. The team must provide the Organizer with a valid value-added tax invoice compliant with national tax regulations before claiming the prize; the Organizer will arrange payment upon receiving a compliant invoice, and the entity shall complete its own tax filing and payment.

(2) Where the winning team does not compete under the auspices of any institution, company, or other entity, or where, despite having an auspicing entity, the prize is paid to a team member's personal account: the Organizer will, in accordance with the tax laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China, fulfill its individual income tax withholding obligations for prizes paid to individuals, and will pay the after-tax prize to the personal account of the team member designated by the team. The team shall, before claiming the prize and as required by the Organizer, provide member identity information, a prize allocation plan, and other materials required for withholding; the team bears sole responsibility where incomplete or inaccurate information provided by the team makes withholding impossible or creates tax risks.

(3) The allocation of prize money among team members is decided by the participating team itself and is no concern of the Organizer; once the Organizer has completed payment of the prize in accordance with this Clause, it is deemed to have fulfilled its payment obligation. Any dispute arising from the allocation of prize money shall be resolved by the team members themselves, and the Organizer accepts no liability.

The tax treatment of prizes awarded in non-cash form is governed by applicable law and the rules of the competent authorities. The Organizer provides no advice on the tax treatment of prizes.

7.3 No Employment Relationship. Participation and winning do not create any employment relationship, hire, sponsorship, partnership, or commercial commitment; the Organizer is under no obligation to offer Participants any subsequent opportunities.

Clause 8 — Attribution and Publicity

8.1 Attribution in Results. By participating in this Competition, the participating team agrees that the Organizer may identify the team by team name and team member names on the Poreva Open platform (including challenge pages) as part of the Published Materials. The team name and member names to be attributed shall be provided by the team at registration or submission as required by the platform; any change must be requested by the captain via the official email and confirmed by the Organizer, and in principle will not be changed after results are published.To facilitate event collaboration, the organizer may provide the names of participating teams, the sequence of submitted designs, and the corresponding experimental validation conclusions to the event partners, but will not proactively provide the personal identity information of team members along with the aforementioned materials.

8.2 Other Publicity. The Organizer will identify a participating team by team name or team member names in advertising, promotional, or other publicity materials relating to this Competition (such as official-account articles, press releases, or social media posts) only with the team's permission; such permission is given by the captain at registration or by email to the official mailbox, and the captain shall ensure that the consent of all team members has been obtained; the permission may be withdrawn at any time, and withdrawal does not affect content already published.

8.3 Design Names. Design names are published as submitted. A participating team should include personally identifiable information in a design only if it is willing for that name to be publicly displayed.

8.4 Institutional Names and Logos. Use of the name, logo, or other trademarks of the institution or company to which a participating team or its members belong requires the consent of that institution or company, which the participating team is responsible for obtaining. This Clause does not grant the Organizer any right to use any institutional name or logo.

Clause 9 — Personal Information Protection

9.1 Processing Principles. The Organizer will process the personal information of participating team members (including the captain)—including information provided during registration, submission, review communication, and award distribution—in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, propriety, necessity, and good faith, and solely for competition organization, identity verification, review communication, results publication, award distribution, and fair-play review; the scope of collection is limited to the minimum necessary to achieve the above purposes. The captain shall ensure that each member's consent has been obtained for the provision of team members' personal information.

9.2 Cross-Border Transfer and Security. For team members whose place of residence is outside China, the transfer and processing of their personal information to and by the Organizer in mainland China must comply with the laws of their place of residence and the regulations of the People's Republic of China on cross-border data transfer. The Organizer will take reasonable security measures commensurate with the risks to protect personal information.

9.3 Rights of Team Members. Team members have the right, in accordance with the law, to access, copy, correct, and delete their personal information, and may withdraw consent in accordance with the law; the means of exercising these rights are set out in the “Poreva Open Privacy Policy”. Cancellation of a team account is requested by the captain via the official email; account cancellation does not affect Published Materials already published under Clause 6.

9.4 Entrusted Processing. The Organizer may entrust third parties with processing personal information on its behalf to the extent necessary; the parties bear corresponding responsibilities in accordance with applicable law.

Clause 10 — Confidentiality

Except for information published as competition results under Clause 6, any non-public information disclosed by the Organizer to a participating team in connection with this Competition (such as target or assay details not yet public before the release of a round's challenges) is confidential information, which the participating team may use solely for the purpose of participating in this Competition and shall ensure that team members who become aware of it are bound by this Clause to the same extent. This Clause does not require a participating team to withhold information it is obligated to disclose under applicable law, but to the extent permitted by law, the team shall give the Organizer reasonable notice before disclosure.

Clause 11 — Data Use and Research

11.1 Aggregated Research. The Organizer may conduct and publish anonymized, aggregated research and analysis on competition results, including comparative methodology studies.

11.2 Third-Party Tool Data. Data generated by a Participant's use of third-party tools or platforms is governed by those third parties' own terms; the Organizer assumes no obligations in respect of such data.

Clause 12 — Cancellation, Suspension, or Amendment

The Organizer reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to cancel, terminate, modify, or suspend this Competition (including any challenge, target, schedule, or award), including to correct errors, respond to force majeure, or address circumstances affecting the fairness of the Competition. Material adjustments to the schedule or rules will be notified via platform announcement. Except as otherwise provided by applicable law, you may not bring any claim, demand, or action in respect of the foregoing cancellation, suspension, or amendment. If the Competition is cancelled mid-course for reasons attributable to the Organizer, the Organizer will properly handle submitted content and make fair follow-up arrangements by platform announcement.

If the impact of force majeure persists for more than 60 days, the Organizer may terminate this Competition, and neither the Organizer nor the Participants shall bear liability for breach.
